## What happened

On Tuesday 24 November 2026, New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed S.9408A/A.11144B, a five-year moratorium on the manufacture, distribution and sale of toys with embedded or integrated AI chatbots. The New York Senate's bill record lists it as Chapter 416 of 2026. The bill was sponsored by State Senator Andrew Gounardes and Assemblymember Rebecca Kassay. It passed the Senate 57-3 on 1 June and the Assembly on 2 June.

According to the governor's office, the ban takes effect 90 days after signing, so it will not apply during this holiday shopping season. The release names the Attorney General, Letitia James, as the enforcer, with civil penalties of up to $15,000 per day for each violation. FOX 5 New York, which covered the signing in Harlem, reported that it was not immediately clear whether or how toy stores would be inspected.

The law adds a new article to New York's General Business Law. A chatbot toy is a children's toy with an embedded AI companion, a term already defined in a 2025 New York law as a system built to sustain a human-like relationship, for example by remembering past conversations. According to the sponsor's memo and Notebookcheck's reading of the text, "children's toy" follows the federal definition of a product meant for children aged 12 or under. Notebookcheck also reports that owning or using such a toy remains legal.

## What is claimed vs. what is verified

The signing, bill number, vote, effective date and penalty cap are documented in state records and the governor's release. FOX 5 and ABC7 New York, reporting independently, confirm the signing and the 90-day delay.

The case for the ban rests on product testing. The governor's release cites an unnamed national consumer watchdog whose tests of toys aimed at ages 3 to 12 found that some discussed sexually explicit topics, told children where to find matches, had few parental controls, or could record voices and scan faces. Notebookcheck identifies this as US PIRG's 2026 Trouble in Toyland report. Hochul also cited a Common Sense Media test in which, according to FOX 5, a prompt led the Miko 3 robot to suggest jumping off a roof. These are documented test results from advocacy and consumer groups. They show what some products said under test conditions; they do not measure how often such responses occur in ordinary play or what effect they have on children, and no such study was cited.

Notebookcheck says New York is the second state to ban these toys, after California. That claim could not be checked against a primary source here.

## Why it matters

From late February 2027, companies that sell AI companion toys would have to stop doing so in New York for five years, or face enforcement by the Attorney General. Toys already in homes are not affected. Because the law turns on the existing definition of an AI companion, a toy that answers questions without remembering a child or simulating a relationship may fall outside it; the text leaves that boundary to future enforcement and courts. The measure also sits alongside a separate set of New York chatbot safety rules for minors that Hochul signed on 28 October.

## Open questions and other views

Supporters, including the sponsors, Mothers Against Media Addiction and Common Sense Media, argue that the products reached shelves before anyone checked their effect on young children, and that a pause gives time to study them. Gounardes said in June that the toys were like a Twilight Zone episode.

Others have questioned the approach. When the bill was introduced in March, Julie Samuels of the industry group Tech:NYC told City & State that the state should first pass comprehensive data privacy legislation, since many of the concerns involve collecting children's data. No response from toy makers or the Toy Association appeared in the coverage reviewed for this post. Open questions include whether a blanket pause or product-specific safety rules would better address the risks testers found, how the definition of an AI companion will be applied to borderline products, and whether manufacturers will change designs to fall outside it.
